[0.23.0] - 2026-11-20
- Full rebranding from FreeSynd to ReSyndicated

[0.22.0] - 2026-11-18
- Aligned V1–V3 cybernetic IPA effect consumption (~9.14x at V3) and dependency regeneration (~6.5x at V3) pacing with the original DOS *Syndicate* wraparound tick curves, and preserved sub-millisecond remainders across per-frame ticks to eliminate V3 timing drift.
- Improved off-road to on-road vehicle pathing so parked or off-road cars evaluate reachable road lanes across multi-lane streets and glide directly into the aligned travel lane without entering wrong-way lanes or making unnecessary corner reversals.
- Fixed vehicle cornering sprite flicker during turns (especially right/down to left/down) using path lookahead steering and moving sprite-sector hysteresis, with expanded Catch2 test coverage in `ipatest.cpp` and `vehicletest.cpp` and synchronized documentation in `docs/CHARACTERS.md`.
- Fixed NPC initial IPA loading (`Adrenaline`, `Intelligence`, and `Perception`) to read active effect and preset lock overrides from mission data rather than neutral midpoints, restoring authentic extended detection ranges for high-Perception Police, Guards, and Civilians.
- Aligned Civilian panic detection radius with Perception scaling (1,500 neutral baseline up to 2,560 at max Perception), added panic triggers for direct attackers beyond sight range, and preserved flee hysteresis until clear of threat range.
- Implemented variable Police disengagement cooldown (~0.75–2.6 seconds based on Adrenaline and encounter timing) when an engaged suspect holsters their weapon, allowing officers to continue pursuit and fire before standing down (and stay engaged if the suspect re-arms), per original Syndicate game behavior.
- Capped scripted guard ambush trigger evaluation at the 15-tile awareness radius so high-Perception guards do not detect players through distant walls.
- Updated `docs/CHARACTERS.md` and expanded Catch2 test coverage for NPC IPA detection/panic scaling and Police disengagement cooldown.

[0.21.0] - 2026-11-18

- Updated flamethrower projectile stepping, Chest cybernetic burn/bullet mitigation and regeneration timing, IPA Perception/Intelligence scaling, and weapon impact ignition on trees, along with synchronized specs in `data/ref/weapons.dat`, `data/ref/characters.dat`, `docs/WEAPONS.md`, and `docs/CHARACTERS.md`.
- Fixed combat recoil damage handling so subsequent hits continue inflicting damage during knockback recoil, matching original DOS Syndicate weapon time-to-kill.
- Added unfenced void recoil transitions and progressive sinking animations (`sinkAnimation`) when pedestrians are knocked off map edges.
- Updated mission binary export to rebuild per-tile spatial entity linked lists for compatibility with the original DOS Syndicate executable.
- Expanded Catch2 unit test coverage across mission export, recoil/void physics, flamethrower/tree ignition, and IPA/cybernetic mechanics.
