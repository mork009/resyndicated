## 1. Briefing Summary

SPRING CLEANING.

COMBAT SWEEP.

AN UP AND COMING TOWN HAS JUST ATTRACTED THE ATTENTION OF HEAD OFFICE AS BEING A VERY PROMISING BASE FOR OUR MARKETING BOYS TO EXPERIMENT WITH.  IT IS OCCUPIED AND DEFENDED BY SEVERAL SYNDICATE SQUADS AND OBVIOUSLY WE CANNOT MOVE IN UNTIL THEY ARE REMOVED.

YOUR SQUAD MUST SWEEP THROUGH THE CITY AND WIPE OUT ALL TRACE OF THE ENEMY SCUM.

**CITY UPDATE:**

THE CITY ITSELF IS DIVIDED IN TWO BY A RAIL LINE. THE ONLY WAY TO CROSS BETWEEN THE TWO IS BY A SHUTTLE TRAIN THAT IS VERY QUICK AND EFFICIENT.

**SYNDICATE UPDATE:**

THE LATEST REPORTS SEEM TO INDICATE THAT THERE ARE AT LEAST THREE ENEMY DEFENCE SQUADS. WE ALSO BELIEVE THAT THEIR SPY NETWORK KNOWS THAT WE ARE COMING. THEY WILL THEREFORE BE READY FOR YOU AND YOU SHOULD EXPECT STIFF RESISTANCE.

**TACTICS:**

THE FULL SQUAD SHOULD BE DEPLOYED FOR THIS ONE. ONE OR TWO OF THE AGENTS SHOULD BE EQUIPPED WITH SUPPORT WEAPONRY AND DRAG BEHIND THE OTHERS WHEN THEY MOVE. THIS WILL ALLOW THEM TO PROVIDE COVER FIRE FOR THE FRONT LINE MEN. TRY TO CLEAR THE FIRST SIDE OF AGENTS BEFORE MOVING THE WHOLE SQUAD OVER TO THE OTHER SIDE. DO NOT SPLIT YOUR SQUAD BETWEEN THE TWO HALVES AS THEY WILL THEN BE EASY PICKINGS FOR THE ENEMY.

## 8. Level Logic & Exceptions

* **Grid Tile Index Alignment**: All tile coordinates $(X, Y, Z)$ are normalized to 0-indexed map tile grid coordinates to align 1:1 with in-game debug displays.

* **Reserved Ped Slots 4–7**: Ped slots 4 through 7 are reserved for persuaded agents during gameplay and are excluded from initial level entity spawning.

* **Scenario Script Command Support**: Executed scenario steps cover Walk / Drive (`1`), Enter Vehicle (`2`), Timed Pause (`5`, `ticks * 100ms` or `5,000ms` default), Leave Vehicle (`6`), Target Escape (`7`), Proximity Ambush Trigger (`8`, 6-tile radius), Loop / Reset (`9`), and Train Station Stop (`10`, `10,000ms` dwell). Scripted NPCs ignore ambient gunfire panic, but bail out and abandon their script if their vehicle is damaged or carjacked.

* **Enemy Agent Tactical AI**: Enemy syndicate operatives do not use waypoint routes; combat activation, target selection, and cross-faction warfare are governed by Section 5's AI Tactics FSM (`AITacticsBlock`).

* **Level Data Adjustment**: Destination coordinates for Police Ped #192 are adjusted (`tile Y` / `Z = 3`) to ensure valid walkable terrain.

* **Dynamic Train System**: Siberia features two multi-carriage trains with dynamic locomotive-to-carriage proximity linkage, persistent driver assignment (Ped #24 in Car #0), station platform boarding (~550 world units), platform-height alighting (`track Z + 1`), and destroyed-segment immobilization.
